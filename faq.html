First Visit

Your first visit is about answers, not adjustments. We start with a one-on-one consult covering what you're dealing with, what you've already tried, and where you want to be.

Then a detailed exam — plus X-rays if needed — so we can see what's really going on structurally.

We don't rush into treatment on day one. Instead we schedule a follow-up to walk you through exactly what we found, explain the root cause, and lay out a clear plan. You leave knowing what's going on and what to do next.
