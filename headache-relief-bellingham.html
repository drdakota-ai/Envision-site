If you've been living headache to headache — reaching for the ibuprofen, powering through, hoping the next one holds off — there's something most people are never told: the majority of recurring headaches actually start in the neck. Here's the cycle we break every week: a headache shows up, you take something, it dulls, it comes back. Nothing ever addressed why the headache keeps happening. That's because pain relievers work on the symptom — not the source. When the source is mechanical (and it very often is), there's a better answer.

Which Type Of Headache Do You Have?

Effective relief starts with the right diagnosis — because the fix depends on the type. The headaches we most commonly help:

  • Tension headaches — the band-around-the-head pressure driven by neck and shoulder tension
  • Cervicogenic headaches — pain that originates in the neck and refers up into the head
  • Migraines — often with a neck component that, when addressed, can reduce frequency and intensity

Why The Neck Is So Often The Culprit

The nerves at the top of your neck share pathways with the nerves that supply your head and face. When the upper cervical joints are stiff, misaligned, or surrounded by tight muscle — from posture, tech neck, stress, or an old injury — that irritation can refer pain straight into your head. Fix the neck, and the headaches often fade with it.

Our Headache-Freedom Approach

  1. Diagnose the pattern. A focused exam identifies whether your headaches are tension, cervicogenic, migraine, or a mix.
  2. Release the source. Gentle upper-cervical adjustments and soft-tissue work relieve the joint and muscle tension referring pain into your head.
  3. Calm the system. Laser therapy reduces inflammation in irritated tissue.
  4. Prevent the next one. Postural rehab and lifestyle guidance keep the trigger from rebuilding.
