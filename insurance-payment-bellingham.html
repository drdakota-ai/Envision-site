TRANSPARENT PRICING

We've intentionally kept our payment model simple and transparent — so you always know what you're paying and why. Here's exactly how it works at Envision Chiropractic.

Why We're Out-of-Network

We are not in-network with insurance, and that's a deliberate choice. Being out-of-network lets us focus fully on the quality and results of your care — your treatment plan is built around what you need, not around what an insurance company will or won't authorize.

Superbills For Reimbursement

If you'd like to use your insurance, we're happy to provide a superbill — an itemized, coded receipt you can submit to your insurer. Depending on your plan's out-of-network benefits, you may be reimbursed a portion of what you paid.

HSA & FSA Welcome

Many patients pay using their HSA or FSA funds, which typically cover chiropractic care. Cash, check, and all major cards are accepted too.

The $47 First Visit

Your first visit — consultation, exam, and any needed X-rays — is just $47 (normally $247). Before any ongoing care begins, we'll walk you through the cost and expectations clearly, so there are never surprises.

Car Accident & Auto Injury

Injured in a collision? Auto-injury care is often handled through Personal Injury Protection (PIP). We're experienced with the documentation these claims require — visit our auto injury page to learn more.

Questions About Cost?

Just ask — we're glad to explain. Call (360) 996-6505 or read our FAQ for more, and see what to expect on your first visit.
