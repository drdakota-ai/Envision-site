Legal & Compliance

NOTICE OF
NONDISCRIMINATION.

EFFECTIVE · JAN 2026
SECTION 1557 · ACA
LANGUAGE ASSISTANCE

Envision Chiropractic complies with applicable federal and Washington State civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, or religion.

Our Policy

We do not exclude people or treat them differently because of any protected characteristic. Every patient who walks through our doors receives the same respect, dignity, and quality of care.

Accommodations & Language Assistance

We provide reasonable accommodations for people with disabilities and, where required, free language assistance services to people whose primary language is not English. If you need these services, please contact us.

Filing a Concern

If you believe we have failed to provide these services or discriminated in any way, you may file a concern directly with our office. You also have the right to file a civil rights complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights.
