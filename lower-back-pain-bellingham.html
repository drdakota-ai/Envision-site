Lower back pain is the single most common reason patients walk through our doors — and the one we get the most dramatic results with. At Envision Chiropractic in Bellingham, we don't chase the ache. We find what's actually causing it, and we fix that.

Here's the truth most people learn the hard way: "lower back pain" isn't a diagnosis. It's a symptom with a dozen possible causes — and the treatment that works for a herniated disc is completely different from what works for a facet-joint problem or muscular instability. That's why the stretch-and-hope, pill-and-wait, "just rest it" approach so often fails. Nobody found the cause.

What's Actually Causing Your Lower Back Pain?

Most of the lower-back cases we treat trace back to one of these. Click through to the dedicated page for yours:

  • Herniated disc — when a disc's inner material pushes out and presses on a nerve
  • Bulging disc — a disc that's swollen or protruding but not yet ruptured
  • Sciatica — pain that radiates from the lower back down the leg
  • Spinal stenosis — narrowing of the spinal canal that pinches nerves
  • Degenerative disc disease — age- or wear-related disc breakdown
  • Posture- and sitting-driven strain — common in desk workers

How We Find The Root Cause

  1. A real consult. We start by understanding your history, your work, your activity, and exactly how and when it hurts.
  2. A thorough exam. Orthopedic and neurological testing — plus digital X-rays when indicated — to pinpoint the structure responsible.
  3. A plain-language explanation. We show you what we found (often using your own images) so you finally understand what's going on.

How We Fix It

Once we know the cause, your plan draws from the right combination of our services:

  • Chiropractic adjustments to restore motion and alignment
  • Spinal decompression to relieve pressure on discs and nerves
  • Laser therapy to reduce inflammation and speed healing
  • Postural rehab to rebuild support so the pain stays gone
