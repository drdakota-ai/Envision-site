The Founder's Story

DR. DAKOTA
TURGEON

Bellingham native. Western Washington high-school athlete who hit college and discovered that the back pain he'd been told to "just stretch out" was a structural problem nobody had ever bothered to actually examine.

That experience — being dismissed, then finally seeing what good chiropractic could do — became the blueprint for Envision. A graduate of Life Chiropractic College West, Dr. Dakota trained in Chiropractic Biophysics, advanced decompression, and laser therapy specifically because he wanted tools to fix things, not just manage them.

He opened the doors of Envision Chiropractic in 2025. Today the practice has grown to over a hundred raving five-star reviews — the vast majority of which use one phrase: he actually listens.
