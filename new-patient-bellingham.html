Walking into a new chiropractor's office shouldn't feel like a mystery. Here's exactly what happens on your first visit at Envision Chiropractic — so you know what to expect before you ever sit down.

Step 1 — The Conversation

We start by actually listening. You'll tell us what's going on, how long it's been happening, what you've already tried, and what you want to get back to doing. This is the step most clinics skip — and it's the one our patients mention most in their reviews.

Step 2 — The Exam

Next is a thorough physical exam — orthopedic and neurological testing — plus digital X-rays when they'll actually change how we treat you. We're building a clear picture of what's causing your problem, not guessing.

Step 3 — The Plan

Then we show you what we found, often using your own images, and explain it in plain language. You'll leave understanding your situation and exactly what we recommend — including realistic timelines.

Your First Visit Is Just $47

The whole thing — consult, exam, and any needed X-rays — is $47, normally $247. No commitment, no pressure, no surprise charges. It's the easiest way to finally get answers.
