Non-Surgical

Spinal Decompression

Computerized traction that gently separates the vertebrae, letting herniated and bulging discs retract — and stubborn nerve pain finally let go. Available for both lumbar and cervical spine.

  • Herniated & bulging discs · sciatica · stenosis
  • Painless, drug-free, surgery-alternative protocol
  • 20–30 min sessions, typically 12–20 over 6 weeks
