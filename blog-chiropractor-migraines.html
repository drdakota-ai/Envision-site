Can a Chiropractor Actually Help Migraines?

Dr. Dakota Turgeon · May 8, 2026 · 4 min read

Let's be honest and specific, because migraine sufferers have heard a lot of overpromising. Chiropractic isn't a cure for migraine. But for a large share of sufferers, it can meaningfully reduce how often and how hard migraines hit — by addressing a trigger most people never get treated.

The Part Everyone Overlooks: The Neck

Migraine is complex — genetics, hormones, sleep, diet, and stress all play roles. But there's a mechanical contributor that routinely goes unaddressed: the upper cervical spine. The nerves of your upper neck feed into the same pain-processing system involved in migraine. When those segments are restricted or irritated, they can lower your threshold — making attacks easier to trigger and harder to shake.

What The Care Actually Does

We're not "cracking your neck and hoping." The approach is targeted:

  • Gentle upper-cervical adjustments to restore motion and reduce a key trigger
  • Laser therapy to calm irritated tissue
  • Postural rehab to reduce the chronic load feeding your attacks

For many patients, the result is fewer migraine days and less intense attacks. Not magic — just removing a real, treatable contributor.

What It Won't Do

We won't tell you to stop your medication — that's a conversation for you and your prescribing physician. We work alongside your medical care, not in place of it. And we'll be honest if we don't think we can help your particular pattern.

Our job is to remove the triggers we can actually treat — and tell you the truth about the rest.
